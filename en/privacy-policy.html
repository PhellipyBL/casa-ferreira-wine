Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Casa Ferreira Wine ("we") respects your privacy and complies with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law nº 13,709/2018). This Policy describes how we collect, use and protect your information when you visit casaferreirawine.com.br.

1. Who we are

Casa Ferreira Wine
Wine tourism project by Dimônaco Holding, based in Bananeiras/PB, Brazil.
Address: Zona Rural, Bananeiras, Paraíba, 58220-000, Brazil
Email: contato@casaferreirawine.com.br
official WhatsApp

2. What data we collect

Anonymous browsing data

Via Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel (when configured), including: anonymized IP address, browser type and version, operating system, pages visited and time spent, traffic source.

Direct communication

  • When you click "Book your experience", you are redirected to SmileIn, our booking partner, which collects data under its own policy.
  • When you contact us via WhatsApp or email, we retain the conversation in order to reply.

Protection cookies

Cloudflare (CDN/security) uses technical cookies to identify bots and protect the site against attacks.

3. Why we collect

  • Improve browsing experience
  • Understand site performance and inform product/marketing decisions
  • Reply to inquiries
  • Security against attacks and abuse

4. Legal basis (LGPD art. 7)

  • Consent (art. 7, I) — for analytics tools (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel). They only load after you accept the cookie banner.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX) — for security protection (Cloudflare).
  • Contract execution (art. 7, V) — for booking-related communications.

5. Who we share with

  • Google LLC (Analytics) — anonymous usage data
  • Meta Platforms (Pixel) — only after consent
  • Cloudflare Inc. — protection and CDN
  • SmileIn — external booking system, with its own policy

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Your rights (LGPD art. 18)

  • Confirm the existence of data processing
  • Access your data
  • Correct incomplete or outdated data
  • Request anonymization, blocking or deletion
  • Request portability
  • Withdraw consent (clear browser cookies or request via email)
  • File a complaint with ANPD (Brazil's National Data Protection Authority)

To exercise any right: email contato@casaferreirawine.com.br or our official WhatsApp. We reply within 15 days.

7. Retention

  • Consent cookie (ours): 30 days
  • Google Analytics cookies: 14 months
  • Meta cookies: 90 days
  • Cloudflare cookies: up to 30 days
  • WhatsApp/email messages: as long as necessary for service

8. Security

We adopt reasonable technical measures: HTTPS with TLS 1.2+, Content Security Policy (CSP), Cloudflare as a DDoS protection layer, multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts, and cryptographically signed source code commits.

Nevertheless, no internet transmission is 100% secure. In case of an incident involving your data, we will notify you and ANPD as legally required.
