The three acts

The Experience

Each afternoon at Casa Ferreira unfolds in three acts: history, terroir and table.

Act I

The Story

Before the first glass, the first word. Sit in the shade of the House and listen to the Ferreira family saga unfold across generations, while a sparkling wine punctuates each turn of the narrative, paired with a carefully composed cheese and charcuterie board.

Act II

The Terroir

Here the soil speaks. Discover the terroir of the Paraíba Highlands and the four grape varieties chosen to be planted in this land, as the white wine tasting unfolds, translating Bananeiras in every sip.

Act III

The Table

The afternoon finds its closing at the table. Main course and dessert, paired with red wine and narrated by the chef, each gesture a signature that extends the memory long after the last glass.
